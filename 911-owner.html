Crown SOS

Private emergency profile and secure SOS sharing

Connect to Crown SOS to manage your emergency profile.

Sign In Or Create Your Account

Only you can manage the saved emergency profile. Recipients of a valid SOS link do not need an account.

Emergency Information

Put all information you want a trusted SOS recipient to see into this profile. The SOS link returns the complete saved JSON object, not just location. Do not include passwords or account secrets.

Secure SOS Link

The link grants access to the entire saved emergency profile plus the latest location shared in this session. Anyone who obtains the link can access that information until it expires or you revoke it.

Live location requires browser permission and an open page. If the device goes offline, the recipient sees the last update and its timestamp, not a guaranteed live position.
