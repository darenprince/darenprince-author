VoxVector

An auditable vocal and audio intelligence platform for structured evidence collection, acoustic and speech analysis, reliability assessment, and controlled deception research.

VoxVector is currently a functional observational research foundation. It is not a scientifically validated deception detector and does not currently produce a validated deception probability or deception verdict.

Analysis architecture

Eligibility and reliability → evidence collection and analysis → candidate classification → final disposition.

The current classifier is indeterminate only and fails closed to abstention or insufficient evidence.

Current analytical scope

The primary pipeline currently integrates RMS and intensity, zero crossing rate, spectral centroid and spread, fundamental frequency and harmonicity, F0 and intensity dynamics, harmonic to noise ratio, spectral flux and rolloff, formant candidate tracking, pause topology, optional response latency, optional transcript disfluency observations, and optional within speaker baseline deviations.

Evidence principles

  • Reliability is an eligibility control, not a deception score.
  • Observations are preserved with provenance and quality context.
  • Missing data and limitations remain explicit.
  • Alternative explanations remain represented.

Current reusable modules

  • MFCC and cepstral processing
  • Local jitter and shimmer utilities
  • Pulse period utilities
  • Interaction and timing utilities

Validation gate

Future validated inference requires task and population definition, speaker disjoint evaluation, out of sample testing, calibration and uncertainty analysis, reliability and abstention testing, and external replication where feasible.
