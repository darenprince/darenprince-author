Find the evidence behind the voice.

VoxVector is being built to detect and evaluate potential deception in interview and conversational audio through structured, reliability-aware, multimethod evidence analysis.

Instead of turning one nervous pause, pitch change, hesitation, or stress response into a verdict, VoxVector is designed to ask a harder and more useful question: what does the available evidence actually support?

Built for the real question

People do not need another dashboard full of disconnected voice measurements. They need an analytical system that can separate usable evidence from noise, show where evidence converges or conflicts, and remain honest about what the data cannot establish.

Reliability first

Before interpretation, VoxVector evaluates whether the recording and available context are suitable for analysis. Reliability is an eligibility control, not a deception score.

Evidence, not theatrics

Acoustic, prosodic, temporal, spectral, voice-quality, transcript, interaction, and baseline observations are organized into an auditable evidence chain.

Uncertainty stays visible

Conflicting evidence, missing context, alternative explanations, and abstention remain part of the result rather than being hidden behind a dramatic single number.

The VoxVector analytical path

The architecture separates the stages that are often collapsed together in simplistic deception systems.

01

Eligibility

Is the audio technically and contextually usable?

02

Evidence

What measurable vocal and conversational observations are present?

03

Candidate

Do multiple evidence families converge, conflict, or remain indeterminate?

04

Disposition

Has the evidence passed the scientific and reliability gates required for inference?

What the current engine measures

The active pipeline already provides a substantial observational foundation. These measurements are evidence inputs, not individual proof of deception.

Acoustic & spectral

  • RMS and relative intensity
  • Zero-crossing rate
  • Spectral centroid and spread
  • Spectral flux and rolloff
  • Fundamental frequency and harmonicity
  • Harmonic-to-noise ratio

Voice & prosody

  • F0 dynamics
  • Intensity dynamics
  • Formant candidate tracking
  • Pause topology
  • Speech and articulation timing
  • Voice-quality observations

Context & language

  • Response latency when supplied
  • Transcript disfluencies
  • Within-speaker baselines
  • Interaction timing
  • Evidence provenance
  • Reliability and missing-data states

Where the product is going

The roadmap expands the evidence layer first, then advances toward validated deception inference. Research candidates remain visible without being represented as production capabilities.

Deeper audio intelligence

Richer voice-quality and spectral descriptors, glottal-source measures, learned audio representations, temporal models, and stronger recording-condition controls.

Conversational intelligence

Production ASR, alignment, repair and false-start analysis, linguistic structure, consistency analysis, question/answer relationships, and speaker-aware baselines.

Validated deception inference

Task-specific classifiers, calibrated uncertainty, multimethod convergence, explicit abstention, robustness testing, and externally replicated validation before controlled inferential deployment.

Current scientific state: VoxVector is not yet a scientifically validated deception detector. The current runtime provides observational analysis and guarded indeterminate classification. That is a maturity boundary, not a change to the product's purpose.

Designed to earn trust

VoxVector follows the Crown Labs communication standard: intelligent, calm, human, structured, and premium. The product should feel powerful without pretending certainty where the evidence does not justify it.

No individual vocal, acoustic, linguistic, behavioral, emotional, or psychological feature proves deception. VoxVector's intended value comes from disciplined evidence collection, convergence analysis, reliability controls, uncertainty, and validated methodology.
