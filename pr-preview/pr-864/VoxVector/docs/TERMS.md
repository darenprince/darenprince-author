# VoxVector Terms of Use

## Product purpose

VoxVector is an advanced vocal deception analysis research and software system. The product is designed to examine measurable characteristics of spoken communication and organize evidence relevant to deception analysis.

## Analytical limitations

VoxVector does not treat an individual vocal feature as proof of deception. Stress, hesitation, pitch, silence, arousal, emotion, cognitive load, speaking rate, pauses, prosody, linguistic observations, and acoustic measurements can have many explanations.

Current implementation and scientific validation status must be distinguished from the long term product objective. An analysis result should not be treated as an established fact about a person solely because software produced a classification or probability.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for determining whether they have the right to submit a recording and whether use of an analysis is lawful and appropriate for their circumstances.

## No guarantee of outcome

The project does not guarantee that an analysis will correctly identify deception, truthfulness, intent, emotion, or any other human characteristic. Availability, accuracy, and interpretation depend on the implemented methods, recording quality, context, validation status, and applicable product limits.
