# VoxVector Licensing

## Purpose

This document defines prospective licensing and deployment models for VoxVector while preserving Crown Labs ownership and the distinction between observational software and future validated inference capabilities.

## Licensing Philosophy

VoxVector can be structured as both:

- a standalone analytical application
- a modular audio-analysis infrastructure layer

Commercial rights should distinguish the software, analytical methods, documentation, research assets, deployment configuration, and any separately licensed third-party components.

## Consumer and Professional Licensing

Potential professional licensing may provide access to:

- structured audio analysis
- reliability reporting
- evidence and provenance records
- repeatable analysis workflows
- export and reporting functions

Specific feature entitlements should track production-ready implementation status.

## Enterprise Licensing

Potential enterprise agreements may include:

- organization-wide deployment
- API access
- role-based workflows
- audit and provenance controls
- managed infrastructure
- integration support
- service-level commitments

Enterprise contracts must not represent research candidates or unvalidated deception inference as validated performance.

## Research and Institutional Licensing

Research licensing may support controlled access to analytical modules, reproducibility tooling, evaluation workflows, and approved research integrations.

Research agreements should define dataset provenance, permitted uses, publication rights, intellectual property ownership, and validation responsibilities.

## API and Embedded Licensing

A mature VoxVector API may be licensed for embedding into third-party analytical workflows. Embedded use should preserve the canonical result contract, reliability states, provenance, abstention behavior, and scientific-status metadata.

## Third-Party Dependency Boundaries

Open-source libraries, models, datasets, and external services must retain their applicable licenses and attribution requirements. A third-party research method does not automatically become Crown Labs intellectual property merely because VoxVector integrates it.

## Intellectual Property Protection

Commercial structures should preserve ownership of:

- VoxVector software authored by Crown Labs
- original architecture and orchestration logic
- proprietary workflow and result schemas where protectable
- original documentation and product materials
- proprietary evaluation assets where lawfully created

## Long-Term Strategy

The long-term objective is a licensing model that allows VoxVector to operate as a trusted analytical platform, enterprise service, and reusable intelligence infrastructure layer while preserving scientific traceability and intellectual property control.
