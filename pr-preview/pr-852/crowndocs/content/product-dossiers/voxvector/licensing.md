# VoxVector Licensing

## Licensing Philosophy

VoxVector can operate as both a standalone analytical application and a modular audio-analysis infrastructure layer.

Commercial rights should distinguish Crown Labs software and documentation from third-party libraries, models, datasets, and services that retain their applicable licenses.

## Professional and Enterprise Licensing

Potential licensing may include individual or professional subscriptions, organization-wide deployments, API access, audit and provenance controls, managed infrastructure, integration support, and service-level commitments.

Contracts must not represent research candidates or unvalidated deception inference as validated performance.

## Research Licensing

Research agreements may support controlled access to analytical modules, reproducibility tooling, evaluation workflows, and approved integrations. Agreements should define dataset provenance, permitted use, publication rights, and intellectual property ownership.

## API and Embedded Licensing

A mature VoxVector API may be licensed for embedding into third-party analytical workflows while preserving the canonical result contract, reliability states, provenance, abstention behavior, and scientific-status metadata.

## Intellectual Property Protection

Commercial structures should preserve Crown Labs ownership of original VoxVector software, architecture, orchestration logic, original documentation, and proprietary evaluation assets where lawfully created, while respecting all third-party licenses.
