# VoxVector Monetization

## Purpose

This document defines the prospective commercial model for VoxVector while keeping current implementation status separate from future revenue assumptions.

VoxVector is an active development deception detection product. No production revenue, customer base, subscription performance, or validated inference metrics are asserted by this document.

## Core Monetization Philosophy

VoxVector's long-term commercial value is expected to come from a combination of deception-analysis workflows, audio intelligence, enterprise case management, validated inference capabilities, API infrastructure, and institutional licensing.

Commercialization should mature alongside the product. Current observational capabilities can support research and analytical workflows, while validated deception inference should become a higher-value product tier only after the required scientific validation is completed.

## Potential Revenue Paths

- software subscriptions
- professional deception-analysis workspaces
- enterprise licensing
- API and analysis infrastructure usage
- research and evaluation programs
- managed analytical services
- institutional deployments
- specialized validation and integration services
- private or controlled deployments for organizations with sensitive audio workflows

## Subscription Model

Potential subscription tiers may be structured around workflow depth and verified capabilities.

### Professional

Structured audio analysis, reliability reporting, evidence records, provenance, analytical exports, and research-oriented workflows.

### Advanced

Expanded batch workflows, baseline comparison, richer transcript inputs, analysis history, evidence convergence views, and additional analytical modules as production ready.

### Enterprise

Organization-level access, role controls, case management, auditability, API integration, deployment support, retention controls, and governed analytical workflows.

### Future Validated Detection Tier

A future tier may expose validated deception-classification capabilities, calibrated probability or confidence outputs, and task-specific decision support only after the applicable methods and models satisfy the VoxVector validation program.

Pricing should be established after production requirements, operating costs, support obligations, and validated product capabilities are known.

## API and Infrastructure Opportunities

A mature VoxVector service could expose controlled analysis capabilities through an API for:

- case-management platforms
- interview and assessment workflows
- investigative systems
- research environments
- quality-assurance systems
- media-analysis applications
- Crown Labs ecosystem products

API commercialization must preserve reliability, provenance, abstention, uncertainty, and scientific-status controls.

## Enterprise and Institutional Opportunities

Potential enterprise categories include:

- investigative and analytical organizations
- security and risk operations
- research institutions
- interview and assessment organizations
- forensic and evidence workflows
- media and communications analysis
- regulated analytical environments
- organizations building audio intelligence workflows

Enterprise positioning should clearly distinguish the intended deception-detection product from the current validation state.

## Managed Services

Before full inference maturity, commercially useful services may center on observational analysis, research support, methodology development, evaluation, integration, and controlled pilot programs.

As validated capabilities mature, managed services can expand toward governed deception-analysis workflows without abandoning auditability or abstention.

## Retention Drivers

Potential retention drivers include:

- repeat analytical workflows
- persistent case and run history
- reproducible provenance
- organization-level auditability
- baseline-aware longitudinal analysis
- evidence convergence and conflict history
- API integrations
- expanding validated analytical modules
- institutional knowledge accumulated through repeated use

## Long-Term Commercial Position

The long-term objective is to establish VoxVector as a premium, credible deception detection and audio intelligence platform whose commercial value increases with verified capabilities, validated methods, reliable infrastructure, and enterprise workflow integration.
