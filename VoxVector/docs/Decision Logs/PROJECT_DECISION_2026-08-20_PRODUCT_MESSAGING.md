# 2026-08-20 — VoxVector Product Messaging Direction

## Decision

VoxVector is the end product being built. Customer-facing communication will present VoxVector confidently as an advanced vocal deception analysis platform rather than foregrounding internal development maturity or negative limitation language.

## Product position

The product objective remains end-to-end vocal and audio deception analysis. The full pipeline is the product architecture: ingestion, preparation, speaker processing, transcription, alignment, eligibility, evidence collection, multimethod analysis, synthesis, classification, validation, disposition, and audit.

Research and validation work strengthens the product. It does not redefine the product as a research experiment.

## Communication policy

Customer-facing pages should be:

- confident
- direct
- premium
- technology-forward
- capability-oriented
- focused on the end product

Internal terms such as `planned`, `partial`, `guarded`, `not validated`, `cannot`, `does not yet`, `only observational`, `insufficient evidence`, and similar maturity or limitation language should remain in developer, QA, validation, and repository-side documentation when technically necessary.

Public copy should not invent functionality or results. When a capability is under construction, communicate the architecture and product direction accurately without falsely claiming that an unavailable execution already occurred.

## Resolution

`VoxVector/docs/PRODUCT_MESSAGING_POLICY.md` is the canonical communication guide for customer-facing product language.

`VoxVector/docs/CHATGPT_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and `VoxVector/docs/OPERATING_CHARTER.md` incorporate this direction into the AI and project operating rules.
