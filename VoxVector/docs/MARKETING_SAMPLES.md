# 60-Second Pitch

Every recorded conversation contains more information than a transcript can show.

VoxVector is being built to make that information examinable.

It combines vocal, acoustic, linguistic, temporal, speaker, and multimethod analysis inside one intelligence platform.

Its broader method library contains more than 300 individually defined analytical data points and evidence fields, creating a deep analytical vocabulary for examining recorded human conversation.

The platform doesn't reduce an interaction to one characteristic or one mysterious score.

It brings observations together.

It examines reliability.

It looks for agreement and conflict.

It keeps uncertainty and alternative explanations visible.

Then it helps determine what the evidence supports.

And because VoxVector exposes a canonical API, organizations can use the intelligence inside their own applications and workflows.

**VoxVector isn't just another way to listen to a recording.**

**It's a deeper way to examine one.**
