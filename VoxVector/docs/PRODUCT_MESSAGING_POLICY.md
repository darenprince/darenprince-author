# VoxVector Product Messaging Policy

## Purpose

VoxVector is the end product being built. Customer-facing communication should present the platform as a serious advanced vocal deception analysis product and communicate the breadth of its technology with confidence.

Internal engineering and validation records remain the source for implementation maturity. Public product communication should not read like an internal QA report.

## Public voice

Use:

- confident
- direct
- premium
- modern
- technology-forward
- intelligent
- evidence-oriented
- ambitious
- clear

Prefer strong declarative language.

Examples:

- `VoxVector analyzes speech across multiple evidence families.`
- `The analysis pipeline moves from audio ingestion through speaker processing transcription feature extraction evidence synthesis and classification.`
- `VoxVector combines acoustic prosodic temporal linguistic and contextual evidence.`
- `Built for advanced vocal deception analysis.`
- `Reveal the truth.`

## Keep internal language internal

Developer documentation should contain detailed implementation state QA status validation requirements incidents failure modes and technical constraints.

Customer-facing pages should not foreground those internal records.

Avoid leading with:

- limitations
- caveats
- abstention language
- incomplete-state language
- negative comparisons
- disclaimers about what the product is not
- language that makes the platform sound speculative
- language that reduces VoxVector to a research experiment

Terms such as `planned`, `partial`, `guarded`, `not validated`, `cannot`, `does not yet`, `only observational`, and `insufficient evidence` belong primarily in developer and repository-side records when they are required for technical accuracy.

## Accuracy rule

Confident messaging does not mean fabricated messaging.

Never invent:

- a completed analysis
- a measurement
- a benchmark
- a validation result
- a model result
- a live metric
- a deployment state
- an integration

When a capability is still being engineered, describe the product architecture and intended capability without falsely claiming a completed execution.

## Product framing

VoxVector should consistently be framed as:

**An advanced vocal deception analysis platform.**

The platform is being engineered as an end-to-end system encompassing:

- file upload and ingestion
- audio decoding and normalization
- provenance and integrity
- recording quality assessment
- speaker identification and diarization
- speech segmentation
- transcription generation
- transcript alignment
- eligibility and reliability
- acoustic analysis
- prosodic and voice quality analysis
- temporal and pause analysis
- linguistic and disfluency analysis
- question and answer alignment
- within-speaker baselines
- evidence assembly
- evidence convergence and conflict analysis
- candidate classification
- validation and calibration
- final classification and disposition
- audit and provenance

This architecture should be communicated as a coherent product system rather than a collection of disconnected research experiments.

## Pipeline language

The public pipeline should emphasize the sophistication and completeness of the workflow.

Preferred:

`From file to final disposition.`

`A complete vocal intelligence pipeline.`

`Every stage contributes structured evidence to the analysis.`

`Audio becomes measurable evidence through a sequence of specialized analytical stages.`

Avoid turning every stage into a warning or qualification. Detailed implementation status belongs in the developer view.

## Scientific discipline

Scientific rigor remains mandatory inside the product architecture.

The system must use sound evidence handling and validation practices. Internal documentation must retain the technical detail required to build and validate the product properly.

That rigor should strengthen the product rather than dominate its marketing voice.

## AI instruction

When generating or editing customer-facing VoxVector copy:

1. Lead with the product and its capabilities.
2. Use confident technology-forward language.
3. Describe the complete product architecture when relevant.
4. Keep internal maturity details in developer documentation.
5. Never invent an execution or result to make copy sound stronger.
6. Never weaken the identity of VoxVector as a deception analysis platform.
7. Do not turn ordinary development status into negative marketing language.
8. Preserve scientific integrity in implementation while keeping public messaging focused on the product.
