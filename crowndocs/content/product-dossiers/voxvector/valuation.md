## Investor Readout

The present approximately **$3.50M central strategic assessment** is not a claim that VoxVector is already a validated deception detector or a revenue-producing SaaS business.

It reflects the assembled value of a functional observational research/software foundation, canonical analysis engine, case-centered architecture, result schemas, evidence/provenance controls, QA and operational diagnostics, product workspace, infrastructure, method registry, validation program, documentation, and brand system.

The largest current discounts remain:

- no verified recurring revenue or customer-contract base;
- no independently established substantial rights-cleared proprietary labeled corpus;
- no independently established proprietary validated deception classifier;
- incomplete end-to-end connected MVP proof;
- remaining enterprise security/privacy diligence work;
- scientific validation that remains a future program rather than a current inference capability.

The next valuation inflection is therefore **proof-driven, not feature-count-driven**.

## Strategic Value Drivers

Value increases when evidence improves in areas including:

- reproducible end-to-end case execution;
- provider-backed transcript/diarization artifacts and alignment;
- reliable report/history/reopen workflows;
- exact-revision QA and browser verification;
- enterprise security/privacy readiness;
- paid design partners and repeat usage;
- rights-cleared proprietary data;
- calibrated task-specific model performance with abstention;
- independent replication or external expert review;
- recurring revenue, retention, margin, and enterprise-contract evidence;
- documented chain of title, exclusive rights, or other defensible proprietary assets.

## Maturity-Based Framework

Foundation value reflects software, architecture, documentation, reusable modules, research assets, and brand.

Product value adds production reliability, customer workflows, security, support, and adoption.

Validated inference value adds task-specific scientific validation, calibrated uncertainty, robust abstention, and reproducible out-of-sample performance.

Platform value adds enterprise scale, recurring revenue, ecosystem integration, proprietary data/model defensibility, and durable market position.

These layers must not be collapsed into one score or one unsupported headline number.

## Intellectual Property and Transferability

Valuation depends on what Crown Labs can demonstrate that it owns, controls, can license, and can transfer.

The assessment may credit lawfully owned original software, architecture, orchestration logic, schemas, documentation, internal evaluation assets, and brand/product materials. It must carve out third-party libraries, open-source components, external model weights, datasets, hosted providers, and other assets governed by separate terms.

Publicly disclosed repository material must not be described as a trade secret. Non-public proprietary assets may receive confidentiality/trade-secret treatment only where they are actually kept non-public and protected with appropriate measures.

No additional premium is assigned solely because patents, registered marks, contributor assignments, proprietary model weights, or proprietary datasets might exist. Those assets should be added only after the diligence record establishes the applicable rights.

See [VoxVector Licensing](licensing.md) and the canonical valuation assessment for the fuller proprietary-IP protection framework.

## Valuation Discipline

Implementation, research literature, software tests, provider configuration, successful execution, deployment, and marketing design do not independently establish deception-detection validity or a market valuation.

Future numerical changes must identify:

- the effective date;
- the repository/evidence revision;
- the maturity state being valued;
- the assumptions and comparable basis;
- the new evidence that changed risk or economics;
- what remains unverified;
- whether the change affects current value, a milestone range, or a scenario only.

The canonical current valuation assessment must be updated in the same documentation change whenever a material valuation driver changes.
