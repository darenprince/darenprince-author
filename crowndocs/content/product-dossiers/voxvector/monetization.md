# VoxVector Monetization

## Purpose

This document defines the prospective commercial model for VoxVector while keeping current implementation status separate from future revenue assumptions.

VoxVector is currently an active development research and software foundation. No production revenue, customer base, subscription performance, annual recurring revenue, retention metric, or validated-inference product metric is asserted here.

The current investor-facing valuation posture is synchronized in [VoxVector Current Valuation Assessment](../../../../VoxVector/docs/CURRENT_VALUATION_ASSESSMENT.md).

## Core Monetization Philosophy

Commercialization should follow technical maturity and customer value rather than imply that research capability is already a production deception detector.

The strongest near-term wedge is **auditable vocal/audio evidence intelligence and professional workflow**: case intake, provenance, synchronized audio/transcript review, speaker-aware evidence, observational analysis, reliability states, audit history, research support, and human-reviewed analytical services.

Potential revenue paths include:

- professional software subscriptions;
- team/investigation workspaces;
- enterprise and institutional licensing;
- API usage;
- managed analytical services;
- research and evaluation programs;
- private or controlled deployments;
- OEM/embedded licensing;
- specialized integration and support services;
- future task-specific validated inference offerings only after the scientific evidence supports them.

This sequencing allows VoxVector to create commercial proof before general deception inference is validated.

## Commercial Sequence

### Phase 1 — Observational workflow revenue

Commercialize product capabilities that can be represented accurately without claiming validated deception detection.

Examples include:

- case management and provenance;
- recording-quality and eligibility reporting;
- synchronized audio/transcript workflows;
- speaker/segment organization where provider execution is verified;
- acoustic, temporal, linguistic, and evidence review where implemented;
- audit trails and exports;
- human-reviewed managed analytical work;
- research/evaluation support.

### Phase 2 — Enterprise and integration revenue

After the connected product, security, privacy, support, and deployment evidence is strong enough, expand into:

- organization controls;
- private or governed deployments;
- API integrations;
- institutional retention and audit policies;
- service-level commitments;
- OEM or embedded workflows;
- higher-value implementation/support packages.

### Phase 3 — Validated task-specific inference

Only after defined-task scientific evaluation supports the claim, VoxVector may add premium inference offerings that preserve:

- calibration;
- uncertainty;
- abstention;
- reliability boundaries;
- target-population limits;
- method/model version provenance;
- alternative explanations;
- auditability.

A future validated tier must not be marketed as universal truth detection.

## Illustrative Planning Ranges

The following amounts are **internal planning assumptions from the current valuation audit**. They are not current list prices, booked revenue, customer contracts, quotes, or evidence of willingness to pay.

| Commercial model | Primary value proposition | Illustrative planning range |

Pricing must be validated against actual compute cost, human-review burden, support requirements, implementation effort, security/compliance obligations, sales cycle, willingness to pay, usage, retention, and competitive alternatives before it becomes a commercial rate card.

## Revenue Evidence Required for Revaluation

Commercial valuation should move from planning assumptions toward revenue-based methods only when evidence exists for metrics such as:

- signed customer or design-partner agreements;
- contracted annual contract value;
- recognized or recurring revenue;
- active paid accounts;
- repeat usage;
- renewal and retention behavior;
- expansion revenue;
- gross-margin and compute-cost performance;
- sales cycle and implementation burden;
- customer acquisition efficiency where measurable;
- referenceability and documented workflow value.

A financing cap, market comparable, or modeled ARR target is not a substitute for these operating metrics.

## Enterprise and API Opportunities

A mature VoxVector service could support case-management platforms, research environments, analytical workflows, media-analysis applications, enterprise review processes, and other audio-intelligence systems through controlled APIs and governed deployments.

Enterprise value is likely to increase when VoxVector can demonstrate:

- reliable end-to-end execution;
- stable result contracts;
- audit/provenance continuity;
- customer and role isolation;
- retention/deletion controls;
- documented security/privacy posture;
- predictable workload cost and latency;
- support and incident-response procedures;
- contractually defined data and IP rights.

## Managed Services

Managed services are a practical bridge between engineering maturity and full SaaS scale.

Before validated task-specific deception inference exists, commercially useful services may center on observational analysis, interview/media evidence organization, research support, methodology development, integration, benchmarking, and evaluation.

Human review should be visible in the service definition. A managed analytical report must not be represented as a validated automated lie verdict when the underlying inferential model has not been validated for that task.

## Monetization and Proprietary Asset Creation

Commercial agreements should be designed so successful customer work can strengthen VoxVector without silently appropriating customer data.

Where lawful and contractually authorized, Crown Labs may seek rights to retain de-identified technical metrics, corrections, evaluation artifacts, or other derived information that improves reliability and product development. Raw audio, transcripts, labels, annotations, and case records should not become Crown Labs training property by default.

Any proprietary dataset or model-value premium requires explicit provenance and rights documentation. See [VoxVector Licensing](licensing.md) and the canonical valuation assessment.

## Long-Term Commercial Position

The objective is to establish VoxVector as a credible vocal/audio intelligence platform whose commercial value grows with verified capabilities, reliable infrastructure, proprietary rights-cleared data, task-specific scientific evidence, enterprise integration, and recurring customer value.

The monetization model should remain diversified enough to support subscriptions, enterprise licensing, API/OEM use, research/evaluation, and managed services without forcing the company to overstate unvalidated deception-detection claims in order to generate revenue.
