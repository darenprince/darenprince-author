# VoxVector Monetization

## Purpose

This document defines the prospective commercial model for VoxVector while keeping current implementation status separate from future revenue assumptions.

VoxVector is currently an active development research foundation. No production revenue, customer base, subscription performance, or validated inference product metrics are asserted here.

## Core Monetization Philosophy

Potential revenue paths include software subscriptions, professional analysis workspaces, enterprise licensing, API usage, research and evaluation programs, managed analytical services, institutional deployments, and specialized integration services.

Commercialization should follow technical maturity rather than imply that research capability is already a production deception detector.

## Potential Subscription Structure

Professional plans may provide structured analysis, reliability reporting, evidence records, provenance, and exports. Advanced plans may add batch workflows, baseline comparison, richer transcript inputs, and analysis history as those capabilities become production ready. Enterprise plans may add organization controls, API integration, auditability, deployment support, and governed workflows.

Pricing should be established only after production requirements, operating costs, support obligations, and validated capabilities are known.

## Enterprise and API Opportunities

A mature VoxVector service could support case-management platforms, research environments, analytical workflows, media-analysis applications, and other audio-intelligence systems through controlled APIs and enterprise deployments.

## Managed Services

Before full product maturity, commercially useful services may center on observational analysis, research support, methodology development, integration, and evaluation rather than claiming automated lie detection.

## Long-Term Commercial Position

The objective is to establish VoxVector as a credible audio-intelligence platform whose commercial value grows with verified capabilities, validated methods, reliable infrastructure, and enterprise workflow integration.
