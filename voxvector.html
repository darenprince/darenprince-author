The engine

Not one lie detector. A synchronized evidence stack.

VoxVector is designed as a multi-engine forensic pipeline. Deterministic signal measurements, timing, phonetics, speech representations, narrative structure, contradiction analysis, and confound modeling remain separate long enough to challenge one another before any conclusion is considered.

D10

Signal integrity

Quality, clipping, codec, channel, noise, discontinuity, and processing checks determine what the recording can actually support.

D14

Temporal cognitive dynamics

Question offset, breath, filler, lexical onset, pauses, repairs, and re-answer timing are evaluated against matched conditions.

D15

Phoneme microcontrast

Matched words and phonetic contexts help separate ordinary anatomy and articulation from question-specific voice change.

D18

Claim graph intelligence

Statements become propositions that can be tracked across chronology, repetition, supporting detail, and independent evidence.

D20

Counterfactual speaker model

The system estimates expected behavior for the speaker, sentence, phonetics, channel, and question difficulty before scoring residual change.

D24

Calibration + abstention

When quality, domain fit, baseline sufficiency, or independent convergence is inadequate, VoxVector is designed to say so.

Method

Follow the waveform. Keep the uncertainty.

The original recording remains the source of truth. Every downstream measurement is timestamped, attributable, reproducible, and allowed to disagree with every other lane.

01 · Preserve

Immutable source audio

Hash the original bytes, preserve channels, and never replace evidence with an AI-enhanced derivative.

02 · Align

Transcript, speakers, words, phonemes

Independent transcription and diarization paths feed consensus alignment back to the waveform clock.

03 · Measure

Timing, acoustics, voice quality, representation

Handcrafted and learned features are retained as distinct evidence classes instead of being collapsed into one opaque score.

04 · Compare

Matched baselines and cognitive challenges

Relevant answers are compared with speaker-specific truth, memory, emotional, task, and phonetic controls whenever available.

05 · Challenge

Alternative explanation engine

Anxiety, fatigue, retrieval difficulty, dialect, embarrassment, channel effects, medication, substance effects, and question complexity remain live competing hypotheses.

06 · Converge

Independent-domain fusion

Only multiple sufficiently independent domains may elevate an investigative concern. Missing prerequisites force uncertainty instead of fake precision.

Scientific prime directive

Stress is not deception.

Pitch, silence, hesitation, breathing, tension, rate, disfluency, whispering, and latency can reflect many states. VoxVector treats them as measurements of change first. Intentional deception remains a hypothesis that requires convergence, context, and uncertainty controls.

01No single vocal feature is allowed to prove a lie.
02Factual error, honest memory failure, and intentional deception are separate targets.
03Model confidence is not the probability that a person lied.
04Out-of-domain or insufficient evidence must be allowed to end in abstention.
