System

A measurement engine, not a magic lie detector.

VoxVector records observable changes and converging evidence without treating stress, hesitation, pitch, silence, emotion, or any individual feature as proof of deception.

01 / Signal

Acoustic forensics

F0, intensity, RMS, ZCR, spectral characteristics, MFCC/cepstral features, harmonicity and voice-quality observations.

02 / Time

Temporal dynamics

Pauses, pause topology, speech rate, articulation rate, response latency, turn duration and interaction timing.

03 / Voice

Voice quality

Jitter, shimmer, HNR and related signal-quality observations with explicit reliability handling.

04 / Spectrum

Spectral & formant analysis

Spectral flux, rolloff and frame-level formant candidates provide richer signal characterization.

05 / Language

Disfluency observations

Filled pauses, repetitions, token statistics and disfluency rates are treated as observations, not deception labels.

06 / Baseline

Within-speaker context

Optional independent baselines allow measurements to be compared against the speaker's own established reference.

Architecture

Five gates. No shortcuts.

The runtime preserves the distinction between whether data can be used, what was observed, what those observations might support, and what the system is ultimately willing to say.

01

Eligibility

Signal quality, duration, integrity and contextual reliability determine whether analysis is appropriate.

02

Evidence

Measurements become traceable, neutral evidence with alternatives and uncertainty.

03

Candidate classification

Current boundary remains indeterminate until an inferential model is genuinely validated.

04

Disposition

Supported, not supported, insufficient evidence, or abstain under explicit gates.

05

Audit

Provenance, software version, method registry and QA state remain inspectable.

Methodology

Research expands the toolbox. Validation earns inference.

Published research can identify useful candidate measurements. It does not automatically make a method a validated VoxVector capability.

Scientific boundary: no single acoustic or linguistic feature is treated as a deception signature. VoxVector reports observations, evidence convergence, uncertainty, data quality and alternative explanations.
