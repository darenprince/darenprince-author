A deeper look under the hood.

VoxVector is not built around one microphone feature or one magic number. The platform organizes distinct analysis engines so each contributes measurable observations to the overall evidence picture.

Analysis techniques

What VoxVector actually measures.

The product combines measurable techniques across several dimensions. Research candidates remain separate until they have been implemented, tested and validated.

Pitch & prosody

Fundamental frequency, dispersion, range, percentiles, slope and endpoint change. These describe vocal dynamics across time.

Intensity & energy

RMS energy, relative intensity and intensity dynamics describe changes in vocal energy while accounting for recording context.

Speech timing

Voiced fraction, pause runs, pause duration, speech rate and articulation rate describe the temporal structure of speech.

Interaction timing

Response latency, turn duration and overlap characterize how speakers coordinate during a conversation.

Voice quality

Jitter, shimmer and harmonic-to-noise ratio provide measurable voice-quality observations when usable cycles are available.

Frequency-domain analysis

Centroid, spread, flux, rolloff and cepstral coefficients characterize the frequency content and movement of speech audio.

Formant tracking

Frame-level formant candidates provide a structured view of vocal resonance and articulation patterns.

Disfluency analysis

Filled pauses, adjacent repetitions and disfluency rate can describe speaking behavior when transcript data is available.

Baseline deviation

Median/MAD reference statistics express current observations relative to the same speaker when a suitable baseline exists.
