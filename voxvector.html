Platform

One system. Multiple evidence layers.

VoxVector combines signal processing, temporal analysis, interaction context and transcript observations into one provenance-preserving analysis workflow.

Acoustic intelligence

F0, intensity, RMS, ZCR, spectral features, MFCC/cepstral observations and harmonicity.

Temporal intelligence

Pauses, speech rate, articulation rate, response latency, turn duration and interaction timing.

Voice quality

Jitter, shimmer, HNR and signal-quality controls help characterize the recording and voice.

Spectral & formant analysis

Spectral flux, rolloff and frame-level formant candidates expand the measurable signal picture.

Transcript observations

Filled pauses, repetitions, token statistics and disfluency rates add contextual evidence.

Speaker baselines

Optional independent reference data supports within-speaker comparison without assuming population norms.

Market

Built for teams that need more than a waveform.

VoxVector is designed as an analysis infrastructure layer that can support multiple products and workflows.

Architecture

Five gates. No shortcuts.

VoxVector deliberately separates whether data is usable from what was measured and what the system is willing to conclude.

01

Eligibility

Signal quality, duration, integrity and contextual reliability.

02

Evidence

Traceable neutral observations with uncertainty and alternatives.

03

Candidate classification

Inference stays indeterminate until a model earns validation.

04

Disposition

Supported, not supported, insufficient evidence or abstain.

05

Audit

Provenance, versioning, method registry and QA remain inspectable.
