Platform

One system. Multiple evidence layers.

VoxVector examines recorded audio from multiple perspectives instead of relying on a single vocal characteristic.

⌁

Audio intelligence

Pitch, intensity, RMS, zero-crossing, spectral and cepstral observations turn recordings into measurable features.

◷

Timing intelligence

Pauses, speaking rate, articulation rate, response latency, turn duration and interaction timing reveal how a conversation unfolds.

◉

Voice quality

Jitter, shimmer, HNR and recording-quality controls add another layer of vocal characterization.

⌗

Spectral & formants

Spectral flux, rolloff and frame-level formant candidates expand the measurable audio picture.

≋

Conversation context

Transcript observations, response patterns and interaction timing help place audio measurements in context.

◎

Speaker baselines

Independent reference recordings can support within-speaker comparisons without assuming that one person's normal is universal.
