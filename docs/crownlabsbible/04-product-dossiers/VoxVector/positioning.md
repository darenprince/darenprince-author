# VoxVector Positioning

## Core Positioning Statement

VoxVector is an advanced vocal and audio deception analysis platform built to identify and evaluate evidence associated with deception in interview and conversational audio.

## Product Identity

VoxVector is explicitly a **deception detection product**.

It is a full-stack intelligence platform built around sophisticated vocal and audio analysis rather than a generic audio toolkit with deception as an incidental feature.

The product architecture spans the complete analytical journey from recording ingestion and speaker processing through transcription, specialized analysis, evidence synthesis, classification, validation, and reporting.

## The Core Customer Problem

A transcript tells you what was said.

The recording contains more.

VoxVector exists for the moments when listening once or reading a transcript is not enough. It gives analysts a structured way to examine the voice, audio, timing, language, and conversational context surrounding what was said.

## The Product Advantage

### Analytical depth

The canonical method library contains **more than 300 individually defined analytical data points and evidence fields** across the broader VoxVector architecture.

This breadth is a central product differentiator.

VoxVector is designed to examine conversations through many measurable dimensions rather than relying on one characteristic or one simplistic deception indicator.

The active runtime emits only the subset supported by the current implementation and available inputs. The broader method library defines the depth and expansion path of the platform.

### Multimethod evidence

VoxVector brings multiple analytical perspectives together so evidence can be examined for agreement, conflict, uncertainty, context, and alternative explanations.

### Speaker-aware intelligence

The architecture supports speaker identification, diarization, speaker timing, speaker-conditioned analysis, and within-speaker comparison as those capabilities mature.

### Evidence-centered analysis

The platform preserves the relationship between observations, measurements, quality, provenance, context, and downstream assessment.

### API accessibility

VoxVector is designed not only as an application but as an analytical service.

Its FastAPI backend gives developers a path to integrate VoxVector analysis into their own systems and workflows.
