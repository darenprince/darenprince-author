# VoxVector Positioning

## Core Positioning Statement

VoxVector is an AI-assisted vocal and audio deception detection platform being developed to identify and evaluate evidence relevant to potential deception in interview and conversational audio.

## Product Identity

VoxVector is explicitly a **deception detection product**. It is not merely an audio-analysis toolkit with deception as an incidental research topic.

The current implementation is an observational foundation, while the product roadmap is directed toward validated, task-specific deception inference.

## Market Position

VoxVector operates at the intersection of:

- deception detection research
- audio intelligence
- speech analytics
- forensic-style evidence organization
- behavioral and conversational analysis
- reliability-aware analytical systems
- AI-assisted evidence synthesis

The product is differentiated by making the evidence chain, reliability, uncertainty, convergence, conflict, and alternative explanations visible rather than reducing an analysis to an unexplained score.

## What VoxVector Is Not

VoxVector is not positioned as:

- a generic voice recorder
- a voice changer
- a consumer transcription utility
- a stress detector presented as equivalent to deception detection
- an unvalidated automated truth machine

## What VoxVector Is Being Built To Become

- a structured vocal/audio deception detection platform
- an auditable evidence and measurement system
- a multimethod deception research engine
- a reliability-aware interview analysis system
- a validated task-specific inference platform
- an enterprise API and analytical workflow layer

## Primary User Categories

Potential users include:

- investigative and analytical teams
- security and risk organizations
- forensic and evidence workflows
- interview and assessment organizations
- audio and speech researchers
- enterprise intelligence teams
- developers integrating deception-analysis capabilities through APIs

Target markets should be narrowed as validated capabilities, deployment requirements, and customer research mature.

## Strategic Differentiation

VoxVector emphasizes:

- stage-separated eligibility and inference controls
- explicit reliability states
- method-level provenance
- multimethod evidence convergence and conflict
- within-speaker baseline support
- alternative-explanation analysis
- abstention as a first-class outcome
- reproducible run fingerprints
- preservation of research candidates without confusing them with validated methods

## Scientific Position

The system does not treat stress, pitch, hesitation, pauses, emotion, cognitive load, or any other individual signal as proof of deception. The intended detection system must combine multiple justified evidence sources and undergo task-specific validation.

## Long-Term Positioning

The objective is to establish VoxVector as a credible, premium deception detection and audio-intelligence platform whose inference capability grows through verified methods, validated models, reliable infrastructure, and transparent evidence presentation.
